Arka Access Map

Who sees which tab, and what each role can do, in Targets & KPIs (monthly targets and progress tracking) and Fit to Run (plant readiness: HOTO points, punch points and QC checklists). It follows the rules the server enforces today; a hidden button is never the only protection.

▶
Every "Open" link is the live demo: the real Arka screens with sample data, exactly as that role sees them. Nothing you do there is saved. Switch role at any time from the bar at the bottom of the demo.

1 · Open a role's view

CEO / Management

Leadership and State head logins

Sees everything, changes nothing in Fit to Run. Starts on Pulse in Targets & KPIs and gets a summary dashboard in Fit to Run.

Targets & KPIsFit to Run

Admin · CEO Office

Admin logins; the plan admins in the CEO Office

Runs the monthly plan (Month plan tab) and does everything in Fit to Run, including QC and O&M approvals.

Month planTargetsFit to Run

Head of department (HOD)

Department heads in the plan

Works on their department's targets and the ones it supports; sees Pulse for what they can see.

My tasksPulse

Team member

People a HOD gives targets to

Updates their own targets: status, checklist, proof, remarks. No Pulse.

My tasksTargets

District in-charge · field team

District in-charge, cluster in-charge, CM and PM engineers

Fill HOTO points and QC checklists for their own plants, raise punch points and mark them fixed with an after photo.

Fit to Run

QC inspector

Given per region by an owner

Reviews submitted checklists in their region: approve or return with a remark; certifies a plant fit to run. Opens plants on the QC checklist.

QC queueDashboard

O&M approver

Given per region by an owner

Closes punch points the field team fixed, or sends them back; follows HOTO progress across plants.

O&M approvalsDashboard

One login can hold more than one role, for example a District in-charge for plants who is also a HOD in the plan; they get both sets of tabs. Regional in-charges and Viewers have no demo of their own: they see the field team's screens for their plants, Regional in-charges with HOTO editing only and Viewers read-only.

6 · Special rules

View as

A platform owner can see Arka exactly as any person sees it, with a banner on top. Nothing can be changed while viewing, and every use is logged.

Test logins

Live in a hidden Test department; never appear in Pulse, scorecards or exports; deleted in one click.

Not in the plan yet

A login with no plan role gets a clear message in Targets & KPIs instead of an empty list.

Emails

Sent only for open months, never while viewing as someone, never for test targets.

About the demo: the screens are Arka's own, with sample progress on Fit to Run and sample targets in Targets & KPIs, recorded on 24 Nov 2026. Plant and people names are real; emails and phone numbers are hidden. Downloads and saving are switched off.
